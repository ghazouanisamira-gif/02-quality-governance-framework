# Quality KPI Framework

A KPI should support a management decision, not simply produce a number.

| KPI | Definition | Frequency | Owner | Decision / Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quality Score | Measures compliance with defined quality criteria | Weekly / Monthly | Quality | Identify gaps and improvement priorities |
| Customer Satisfaction | Measures customer perception of service quality | Weekly / Monthly | Operations | Identify customer-impacting issues |
| Compliance Rate | Measures adherence to required standards | Monthly | Quality | Trigger corrective actions |
| Non-Conformity Rate | Tracks identified deviations from requirements | Weekly / Monthly | Quality | Prioritize root cause analysis |
| Audit Coverage | Measures proportion of planned controls completed | Monthly | Quality | Adjust audit plan and resources |
| Corrective Action Closure | Measures timely closure of agreed actions | Weekly / Monthly | Process Owner | Escalate overdue actions |
| Training Completion | Measures completion of required learning activities | Monthly | Training / HR | Close capability gaps |

## KPI Design Rules

1. Define the business purpose.
2. Specify the calculation method.
3. Assign one accountable owner.
4. Set a target or reference level.
5. Define review frequency.
6. Link exceptions to an action or decision.

## KPI Lifecycle

**Define → Measure → Review → Analyze → Act → Reassess**

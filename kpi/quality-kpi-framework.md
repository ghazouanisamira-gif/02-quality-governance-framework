# Quality KPI Framework

A quality KPI is useful only when its definition, owner, review rhythm and management action are clear.

## KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Definition | Frequency | Owner | Typical management use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quality Score | Performance against defined quality criteria | Weekly / Monthly | Quality | Identify quality gaps |
| Customer Satisfaction | Customer perception of service quality | Weekly / Monthly | Operations / Quality | Prioritize customer-impacting issues |
| Compliance Rate | Adherence to defined requirements | Monthly | Quality | Trigger corrective action |
| Non-Conformity Rate | Frequency of identified deviations | Weekly / Monthly | Quality | Focus root cause analysis |
| Audit Coverage | Completed planned controls vs. planned controls | Monthly | Quality | Adjust audit capacity |
| Action Closure Rate | Corrective actions closed within agreed timeframe | Weekly / Monthly | Process Owner | Escalate overdue actions |
| Training Completion | Required learning completed | Monthly | Training / HR | Address capability gaps |

## KPI Design Rules

Every KPI should define:

1. **Purpose** — what management decision does it support?
2. **Formula** — how is it calculated?
3. **Data source** — where does the evidence come from?
4. **Owner** — who is accountable?
5. **Frequency** — when is it reviewed?
6. **Target / threshold** — what constitutes acceptable performance?
7. **Action rule** — what happens when performance deviates?

## Quality KPI Hierarchy

**Outcome KPIs → Process KPIs → Control KPIs → Improvement KPIs**

This prevents a dashboard from becoming a collection of disconnected numbers.

## KPI Lifecycle

**Define → Measure → Validate → Review → Analyze → Act → Reassess**

## Data Governance Note

KPI interpretation requires consistent definitions. Where operating contexts differ, trends should be interpreted with their process, channel, market or provider context rather than used for simplistic comparisons.
